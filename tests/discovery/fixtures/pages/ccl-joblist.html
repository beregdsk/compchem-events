Submitting your entry:

The income from job advertising on the CCL Job Page is used to support hardware, software, development, and maintenance of CCL.NET, the Internet based forum and its Web site. The current prices for this service are listed below. Due to inflation the original prices were adjusted for inflation since 2004 and increased by 50% on March 11, 2023.

  • 150 USD -- if the job is submitted via Web Form Interface. You will be responsible for format and looks of your page. Make sure that you check your spam box if you do not see a response within minutes from submission.
  • 225 USD -- if the job is submitted by e-mail to: jobs@ccl.net, that is, it requires manual formatting and placing on the CCL Web site by CCL personnel. Please note that the spam filters at ccl.net domain are overzealous and they sometimes kill good messages. If you do not get my response within a day please contact me (or submit the ad) via secure web page that allows for uploading files and text.
  • 40 USD -- a surcharge if you choose to pay for posting the ad by the wire/bank transfer. If you pay with a Credit Card, PayPal or a Check/MoneyOrder your cost will be $150 or $225. However, for Bank/Wire transfer, it will be $190 or $265, respectively. Please realize that wire transfer is used to pay to ship containers of stuff rather than petty cash items and CCL in some instances had negative overall income from such payments when they were placed incorrectly by the originating bank.

You will be issued an invoice for payment AFTER your job is posted. At time of submission you choose how long you want the ad displayed (1 day to 6 months, default 3 mos.). The payments can be made by credit card, bank transfer, and check. Details are provided in the invoice and email message that you will receive after job ad is posted. You will also be able to print the W-9 IRS form if your institution needs it. Please click on Details of CCL.NET Job Submission. We encourage you to use this service not only because it is very successful and will bring you good candidates, but also because your support will ensure that the venerable CCL.NET, one of the first Internet based discussion forums, will continue for years to come.
 
Jan Labanowski, Ph.D.
